    Independent devices. Continuing conversations.

    Account control, historical decryption and pending-work recovery have different requirements.

    V1 / Generation 1 — Candidate, PARTIAL. This revision changes the security transcript. Formal proof requalification is required; new-session eligibility is false and the Protocol Line is publication ineligible. Standard Nostr messaging remains a separate upstream contract. Definition tests do not establish deployed client support.

    User authority state

    A self-certifying user authority chain authorizes independent Endpoints. A Nostr account is an external association, not a replacement for that chain and not permission to execute tools.

    Device copies are not execution rights

    Phone and desktop may both retain an authorized message. A work request still identifies its execution target or explicitly authorized executor set. Another device must not repeat an ambiguous external action merely because it received a copy.

    Recovery preserves meaning, not unsafe old sending state

    Recovering account control does not reconstruct deleted history or lost decryption keys. Restore durable work and deduplication facts; establish safe current cryptographic state when required, without changing the visible conversation or authorizing a second effect. Never restore an old counter and reuse its cryptographic material.

    Independent chain catch-up

    Identity, affiliation and peer routes retain their own scope and predecessor validation. Missing history triggers authenticated catch-up, not arbitrary adoption of the largest advertised number. Security-authority forks remain explicit and must not be selected by relay arrival order.

    Changing a route does not change a person

    Choose only authorized recipients and carriers at the required protection level. Native Endpoint association persists independently from temporary Nostr or HTTPS delivery attempts. Revocation restricts new effects without erasing already retained historical facts.
