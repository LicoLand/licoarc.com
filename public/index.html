    LicoArc defines one implementation-neutral Protocol Line for end-to-end encrypted messaging, independent device identity, reliable exchange, groups, and federation across untrusted relay Stations.

    Understand the core concepts

    Eight capabilities.
    One closed line.

    Each mandatory capability is complete in the current definition. The line binds their semantic identities and the active protection profile into one exact content identity.

    1. 01Protocol Foundation

      Representations, closed registries, bounds, lifecycle, and content identity.

    2. 02Identity

      User authority, independent Endpoint devices, recovery, and transparency.

    3. 03Pairwise Protection

      The indivisible hybrid establishment and protected-record profile.

    4. 04Generic Messaging

      Six protected message classes and resumable attachment state.

    5. 05Reliable Exchange

      Stable intent, retries, Endpoint confirmations, and restart convergence.

    6. 06HTTPS Transport

      A bounded HTTP/2 and TLS 1.3 carrier with no security authority.

    7. 07Group Collaboration

      Bounded protected membership, transitions, projections, and partial failure.

    8. 08Federation Governance

      Threshold evidence, distribution binding, equivocation, and local admission.
